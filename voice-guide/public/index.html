$ Monatry TTS Voice selector

Pick a voice,
then say it in brackets.

Every voice below is one the bot can read your chat message in. Play a sample, find one you like, and put its name in square brackets at the very start of your message:

[af_sky] Streamer is washed. My dead grandma could do this in her sleep.

  • The brackets have to come first: anything before them and the whole thing is read out as ordinary text, brackets and all.
  • Type the name exactly as it appears on the card, for example bm_george or jf_nezumi. Click one to copy it.
  • Leave the brackets off and you get your usual voice, so you only need this when you want to switch.
